We are dedicated to improving smallholder agriculture

Agroswift is a unified agribusiness based in Kisumu, Western Kenya, where we also run a manufacturing plant at the KIRDI facility. We currently have offices in Kenya and Uganda. Our operations serve smallholder farmers across the East African region.

We are seeking to improve farmer productivity and ensure timely delivery of safe foods to consumers.

Our relentless mission is to change the world of smallholder agriculture. Our commercial activities positively influence rural prosperity as well as deliver safe and nutritious foods to urban markets.

47 Country Operations
10 Research Themes
300 Member Farmers

We engage with small-scale farmers in adopting sound farming practices and securing sustainable markets for their production.

We're also an agri-advisory firm that lends support to institutions active in smallholder agricultural systems. We help clients build resilient and smallholder-based supply chains, which in turn help farmers grow their farm enterprises profitably.

Our mission

Improving smallholder agriculture. Agroswift employs five measures geared to accelerate deployment of leading innovations into sustainable products and services.

A vision of rural prosperity by supplying nutritious food to urban markets

Agroswift works with 300+ smallholder farmers. We have an established and dedicated extension service for the benefit of our farmers. Our contracted farmers receive high quality advisory services and inputs to improve their productivity.

What we do

Supply and distribution of fresh produce in Kenya and Uganda. Food processing to deliver safe produce and avoid wastage. Distribution of high quality seeds, fertilizers, and agrochemicals. ICT solutions that reduce uncertainty in crop production.

We value technology as a key driver for agricultural transformation in Africa.
