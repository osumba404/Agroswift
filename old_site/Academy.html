Academy

Our training centre offers training, sharing of knowledge about crop and livestock enterprises. As a leading regional training centre with several training sites, students are provided with individualized guidance and advice. From our experienced staff, students receive excellent coaching sessions and practical education.

We seek to foster innovation and enable higher productivity in all farm enterprises. Our academy endeavors to offer opportunities for partnerships in training, research and development. We are open for cooperative partnerships with a view of supporting small holder farming systems. Devolved farmer schools and field trials are a core component of the Academy. Our scope of activities include the following:

  • Peer managed demo activities
  • Village training & farmer field days
  • Market driven production systems
  • Linking education, research and industry
  • Developing extension materials
