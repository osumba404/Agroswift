Our Services

Agroswift is also an Agri-Advisory firm that lends support to institutions active in small holder agricultural systems. We help clients build resilient and small-holder based supply chains which in turn help farmers grow their farm enterprises profitably.

A key objective of Agroswift is to improve the livelihoods of smallholder farmers in Sub Saharan Africa. Our mission is to build capacity by offering expertise in various themes pertaining to successful practices of on-farm and off-farm Agri-activities.

We help farmers break cycles of dependency on rain-fed water, degraded soils and low yielding seeds. Instead, we help farmers adopt Agri and Market Smart (AMS) methods through intensification and diversification of their farm enterprises.

Our services has helped us to develop many partnerships with organizations concerned improving smallholder systems.Our five priority countries of interest are Uganda, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Kenya and Malawi.

“Agroswiftˋs mantra is to learn, walk and grow together. We endeavor to partner, build capacity and grow organically with small holder farmers whose paradigm is shifting to ‘best in class’ agribusiness as opposed to subsistence farming.”

With our four pillar approach mantra, Agroswift has narrowed further to focus its strength of activities in the following pathways:

  • Value chain analysis
  • Agri risk management
  • Training, research and development (Agro innovation)
  • Partnerships and collaborations

These areas of expertise is further broken down into nine different clusters:

  • Small holder livestock enterprises
  • Small holder crop enterprises
  • Conservation agriculture (green farming)
  • Renewable energy
  • Mechanized smallholder agriculture
  • Rural credit services
  • Extension servicesand smallholder farm networks
  • Decision support systems (ICT/fintech)
  • Post-harvest and processing solutions
